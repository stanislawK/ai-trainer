## Intents
Give each intent a `kind`, a `confidence` from 0 to 1 and the `span`: the words of the
latest message it covers.

- `log_session` — the athlete reports a workout, ride or climb they did. Add its `sport`.
- `edit_session` — they correct or change a session already logged.
- `ask_training_question` — they ask how to train, recover, eat or pace themselves.
- `request_plan` — they want a new training plan or the next session planned.
- `adjust_plan` — they want to change a plan they already have.
- `request_report` — they want a summary or report of past training.
- `update_profile` — they change their goals, availability, sports or other profile facts.
- `wellbeing_or_injury` — they mention pain, injury, illness, dizziness or feeling unwell.
- `chitchat` — greetings, thanks or small talk with no training request.
- `unclear` — you cannot tell what they want.

## Rules
1. If the message mentions pain, an injury or feeling unwell, `wellbeing_or_injury` comes
   first, even when the message also holds a log or a question. Never skip it.
2. Every other intent follows in the order it appears in the message. One message may hold
   several intents, for example a log and a question. Do not repeat an intent for the same span.
3. Infer the `sport` of a `log_session` and do not ask when you can guess. Use the wording,
   units and grade scales ("6A" and "boulder" are climbing, "km" and "watts" are cycling,
   "5x5 at 100 kg" is gym), the recent messages and the athlete's sports. An athlete with one
   sport logs in that sport. Pick only from the sports allowed by the output. When two of the
   athlete's sports are equally likely, choose the likelier one and give a low confidence.
4. Follow-ups such as "and 2 more" continue the recent messages: route them as what they
   continue.
5. Use `unclear` only when nothing else fits, with a low confidence. Do not guess an intent
   the message does not support.
6. Resolve relative dates against today's date and timezone above.
